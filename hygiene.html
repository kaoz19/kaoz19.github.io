Personal Hygiene and Prevention

Handwashing Techniques

  1. Wet your hands with clean water.
  2. Apply soap and rub for 20 seconds.
  3. Clean between fingers and under nails.
  4. Rinse and dry with a clean towel.

Dental Hygiene

  • Brush your teeth twice a day.
  • Use floss daily.
  • Eat less sugar and sweets.
  • Visit the dentist regularly.

Personal Cleanliness

  • Take a bath every day.
  • Wear clean clothes.
  • Cut your nails regularly.

Food Safety

  • Wash fruits and vegetables.
  • Cook food properly.
  • Keep leftovers in the fridge.
  • Drink clean water.

Prevention of Communicable Diseases

Communicable diseases like cold, flu and typhoid spread from one person to another. To stay safe, cover your mouth when you cough, wash your hands often and stay home when you are sick.

Importance of Vaccination

Vaccines protect us from dangerous diseases. They help our body fight germs before we get sick.
