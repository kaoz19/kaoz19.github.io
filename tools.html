BMI Calculator

BMI = weight (kg) / height (m) x height (m)

Example: 60 kg and 1.65 m gives BMI = 22

BMI Result
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 to 24.9 Healthy
25 to 29.9 Overweight
30 and above Obese

Daily Health Tips

  • Monday: Drink a glass of water after waking up.
  • Tuesday: Walk for 10 minutes after lunch.
  • Wednesday: Eat one extra fruit.
  • Thursday: Sleep early tonight.
  • Friday: Stretch your body for 5 minutes.
  • Saturday: Play a sport with friends.
  • Sunday: Rest and relax.

Random Health Facts

  • About 60% of your body is water.
  • Washing hands with soap stops many infections.
  • Walking helps reduce stress.
